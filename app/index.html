Genre

Shots

Each shot is one MiniMax H3 clip of about four and a half seconds, cut to the song's bars. Six shots is about 25 seconds of film and an hour of rendering on a 12 GB card.

One line, one film

Say what the film is about. A local Qwen3 writes the shot list and the song brief; YuE2 writes the sheet music and performs it; Krea 2 frames every shot and MiniMax H3 shoots it; SAM 3 tracks the subject and the library fires one effect per shot on the song's own lanes; the sound is synthesized in the song's key; the cut lands on the bars. Open weights, this machine, no prompt-writing. Edit the plan before you make it.
