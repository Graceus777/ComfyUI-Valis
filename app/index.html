What to hide

      
      

      

      

    

    

      

        
Hidden in noise

        
Two ways to hide a moving thing in a field of dots. The stereogram (magic eye): free-view it, parallel or cross-eyed, and the thing stands out of the dots in 3D. The kinetogram: the thing is made of the same dots as the field and only its dots move, so every paused frame is plain noise and it exists only while it moves. A decode check reads the depth back after an H.264 round trip, to see if it survives an upload.

      

      
Nothing hidden yetDrop a clip with a person or a thing that moves.
